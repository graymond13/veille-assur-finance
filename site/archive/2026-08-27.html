1. ​The EBA consults on draft technical standards on institutions’ operational risk management

Publié : 2026-08-26T08:37:24+00:00 · Domaine : eba.europa.eu

​The European Banking Authority (EBA) today launched a public consultation on draft Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS) specifying the operational risk management framework that institutions must have in place as per Article 323 of the Capital Requirements Regulation (CRR3). The draft RTS set out harmonised, proportionate requirements for the governance, management process and systems institutions should use to identify, assess, monitor and manage operational risk. The consultation runs until 31 December 2026.
